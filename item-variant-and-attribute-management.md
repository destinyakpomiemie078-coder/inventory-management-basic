# Item Variant and Attribute Management

**Topic:** Item Identification (SKU Management)

## Short Description
This feature lets the system record one product that comes in different versions (for example size, colour, flavour or capacity) and give every version its own unique SKU. Instead of treating "T-shirt, Red, Large" and "T-shirt, Blue, Small" as two unrelated items, the system groups them under one parent product while tracking each variant separately.

## Purpose
- To identify every version of a product precisely, so the right item is counted, sold and reordered.
- To avoid duplicate or confusing item records.
- To make reports clearer by showing stock for the whole product and for each variant.
- To reduce errors when receiving, issuing and counting stock.

## Main Functions / Activities
1. **Create a parent product** with shared details such as name, brand, category and description.
2. **Define attributes** such as size, colour, weight, model or expiry type.
3. **Generate variants** from combinations of attribute values (for example 3 sizes x 4 colours = 12 variants).
4. **Assign a unique SKU** to each variant, following a consistent naming format.
5. **Set variant-level details** such as cost price, selling price, barcode and reorder level.
6. **Track stock per variant** and show the combined total for the parent product.
7. **Edit, deactivate or archive** variants that are discontinued, without deleting their history.
8. **Search and filter** items by parent product or by any attribute.
9. **Prevent duplicates** by checking that no two variants share the same SKU or attribute combination.


    # Storage Location and Bin Management

**Topic:** Core Store Keeping

## Short Description
This feature defines where items are physically kept in the store or warehouse, such as warehouse, section, shelf, rack and bin. Every item's stock is linked to a specific location, so staff always know exactly where to find it and how much is stored there.

## Purpose
- To make items quick to find, saving time when picking and issuing stock.
- To keep the physical layout of the store and the system records in agreement.
- To use storage space efficiently and avoid overcrowding.
- To make stock counts and audits easier and more accurate.

## Main Functions / Activities
1. **Set up the location structure**: create warehouses or stores, then zones, shelves, racks and bins, each with a unique code (for example STORE1-A-03-B2).
2. **Assign items to locations** when stock is received or put away.
3. **Track quantity per location**, including items split across several bins.
4. **Transfer stock between locations** and record who moved it and when.
5. **Set location rules**, such as capacity limits or restricting a bin to certain item categories.
6. **Search by location** to see everything stored in a shelf or bin.
7. **Show item location** on stock lists and pick lists to guide staff.
8. **Support stock counts** by location so counters can check one area at a time.
9. **Maintain a movement history** for each location for audit and tracing.
